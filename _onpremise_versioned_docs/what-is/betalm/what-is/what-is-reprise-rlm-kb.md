### What is Reprise RLM?

**Reprise's** flagship product is the **Reprise License Manager (RLM)**. It is a software licensing toolkit that controls the allocation of licenses by managing check-in and check-out requests. RLM keeps track of which users and computers can use licenses and, for floating licenses, monitors the number of concurrent copies in use.

RLM was developed by the original team behind FlexNet Publisher at GLOBEtrotter. Using this experience, they designed RLM to be simple and flexible, avoiding some of the complexities found in other license managers.

OpenLM provides the following functionality for monitoring Reprise RLM licenses:

* **Denials Reporting**: Yes
* **Report resolution**: By Second
* **Borrowed License reporting**: No
* **Expiration Date reporting**: Yes
* **Multiple Server Redundancy Support**: No
* **Token License Support**: No

Reprise RLM is one of over 70 license managers that OpenLM can manage.

***

### Who is Reprise?

**Reprise Software** was founded in 2006 in California by developers of GLOBEtrotter, the company that created Flexera's FlexNet. They leveraged their previous experience to develop RLM, a product designed to be simple, flexible, reliable, and secure. Reprise has also launched **RLMCloud**, a hosted license management solution.

With over 600 customers, including prominent companies like Schlumberger, Exa, Lanner, and Tektronix, Reprise is a key player in the license management market.
