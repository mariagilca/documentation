### What is the SolidNetwork License Manager (SNL)?

The **SolidNetwork License Manager (SNL)** is the proprietary license manager for SOLIDWORKS, used at sites with a network license. It's a server-based tool that allows multiple users to share a pool of licenses, providing cost efficiency and flexibility. The license manager must be installed on a license server, and for network customers, it needs to be upgraded with every new release of SOLIDWORKS.

Earlier versions of SOLIDWORKS used hardware dongles for license protection, but modern versions use a software-based system. Entitled clients are identified by loading a license serial number on the client machine. When a machine is decommissioned, the serial number must be deactivated and then reactivated on a new machine. The SNL supports both concurrent user licenses and standalone licenses.

OpenLM provides the following functionality for monitoring SOLIDWORKS licenses:
- **Denials Reporting**: No
- **Report resolution**: By Minute
- **Borrowed License reporting**: No
- **Expiration Date reporting**: No
- **Multiple Server Redundancy Support**: No
- **Token License Support**: No

### Who is SOLIDWORKS?

SOLIDWORKS is a brand of **solid modeling computer-aided design (CAD)** and **computer-aided engineering (CAE)** software. It was founded in 1993 by **Jon Hirschtick**, an MIT graduate, with the goal of creating an affordable, user-friendly 3D CAD application for the Windows desktop. This was a revolutionary concept at the time, as most competing CAD systems were expensive and ran on Unix-based workstations.

The company's first product, **SOLIDWORKS 95**, was released in 1995 at a price point significantly lower than its competitors. Due to its success, the company was acquired by the French software giant **Dassault Systèmes** in 1997. Under Dassault's ownership, SOLIDWORKS has grown to become an industry leader with millions of users worldwide. Today, SOLIDWORKS is used across various industries, including aerospace, automotive, and manufacturing, to design, simulate, and manage the entire product development process.
