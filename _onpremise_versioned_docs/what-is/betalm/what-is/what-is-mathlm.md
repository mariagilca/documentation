### What is MathLM?

**MathLM** is Wolfram's proprietary license manager for its products, which include **Mathematica** and **Wolfram One**. It's designed to be a network license manager for customers running multiple instances of the Wolfram system. MathLM is installed on a single machine, known as the license server, and acts as a gatekeeper for new Wolfram sessions. It monitors the number of concurrent processes and issues licenses until all available slots are filled. The number of concurrent processes allowed is specified on the network license certificate.

OpenLM provides the following functionality for monitoring MathLM licenses:
* **Denials Reporting**: No
* **Report resolution**: By Minute
* **Borrowed License reporting**: No
* **Expiration Date reporting**: No
* **Multiple Server Redundancy Support**: No
* **Token License Support**: No

MathLM is one of over 70 license managers that OpenLM can manage.

### Who is Wolfram?

**Wolfram Research** is an American multinational company that creates computational technology. It was founded in 1987 by **Stephen Wolfram**, a pioneer in the field of mathematical computation. The company's flagship product is **Mathematica**, first released in 1988, which is used by millions worldwide. In 2009, the company launched **Wolfram|Alpha**, an answer engine built on the foundation of the Wolfram Language. Wolfram's corporate headquarters are in Champaign, Illinois, and it has offices on every continent.
