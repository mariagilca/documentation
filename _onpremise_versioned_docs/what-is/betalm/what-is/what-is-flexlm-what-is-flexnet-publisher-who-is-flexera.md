### What is FlexLM?

**FlexLM** is a widely recognized name for **Flexera's** core license management solution. It's the de facto standard in the industry, and many users still refer to the product by this name. The product was officially renamed to **FlexNet Publisher** in 2000.

***

### What is FlexNet Publisher?

**FlexNet Publisher** is the core product within the **FlexNet Licensing** suite. It's a premier software license manager used by many software vendors to manage various licensing models, including **perpetual**, **node-locked**, and **pay-per-use licenses**.

Flexera also offers other licensing products, such as **FlexNet Embedded** for IoT devices and a **Cloud Monetization API**. For vendors who prefer a combination of software and hardware licensing, there is also the **FlexNet ID Dongle**.

OpenLM offers comprehensive functionality for monitoring FlexNet licenses, including:
* **Denials Reporting**: Yes
* **Report resolution**: By Second
* **Borrowed License reporting**: Yes
* **Expiration Date reporting**: Yes
* **Multiple Server Redundancy Support**: Yes
* **Token License Support**: Yes

***

### Who is Flexera?

Flexera is an American software company that specializes in **software asset management** and **cloud management**. Its history dates back to 1988 with the creation of the **FLEXlm** license management software by **GLOBEtrotter Software**. After a series of acquisitions and name changes, the company was ultimately renamed **Flexera Software** in 2009.

Over the years, Flexera has grown through strategic acquisitions to build its product portfolio, including:
* **ManageSoft** in 2010 for its software asset management capabilities.
* **RightScale** in 2018 for cloud management.
* **RISC Networks** in 2019 to complement its cloud offerings.
* **Snow Software** in 2024 to further expand its IT Asset Management (ITAM) and FinOps capabilities.

Flexera is a leading provider in the software license management market, with a global presence, over 2,300 employees, and tens of thousands of customers.
