<h2>What is DSLS?</h2>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">DSLS (Dassault Systemes License Server) is </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">Dassault</span><span style="font-weight: 400;"> Systemes’ proprietary license management application. This tool is in charge of enrolling Dassault licenses on the server; Dassault applications that are installed on end-user workstations communicate with the license server over the network. The license server either grants or denies a license from the client, based on license availability and license agreement compliance.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">One of the major drawbacks of DSLS was that it did not support virtual servers, because they felt it compromised the security of their licenses, despite growing pressure from their customers. This was reviewed in 2018 and DSLS now supports virtual servers, making the life of license managers at a site that uses 3DS products much easier.</span>

<h3>Who is Dassault?</h3>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">French company Dassault was incorporated in the 1970s, when it was best known as an operator in the aviation space, with its iconic Mirage aircraft, adopted and adopted by defense forces across the globe. Through a partnership with IBM to develop CATIA in the late seventies, it has evolved into a major engineering software vendor, supplying solutions to many industries such as Dassault Systemes. Over the years they have acquired many engineering software companies, some of which used different license managers, such as FlexNet, but have their own license manager, called </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">DSLS</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">. Dassault Systemes (3DS) has focused on 3D modeling within the PLM (project lifecycle management) value chain, the “3D Experience”. They have over 250,000 enterprise customers, with about 10 million on-premise and 100 million online users. Products include 3DExcite, 3DExperience, Catia, BioVia, Simulia (formerly Abaqus) and Solidworks.</span>
