<h2><b>What is FlexLM</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">?</span></h2>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">FlexLM is Flexera’s core license management solution, which has been renamed to </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">FlexNet Publisher</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">, a name originally used during 2000-2009.</span>
<h2><b>What is FlexNet Publisher?</b></h2>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">FlexNet Publisher is the core product under the FlexNet Licensing</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">®</span><span style="font-weight: 400;"> umbrella. It is the premier software application for license management, with nearly half the global market. </span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">Software vendors can use it to manage perpetual, node-locked, and pay-per-use licenses.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">Many of our customers are still familiar with the handle “FlexLM”.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">Other Licensing products are:_</span>
<ul>
 <li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">FlexNet Embedded, with a small footprint for IoT devices</span></li>
 <li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">The Cloud Monetization API that accesses FlexNet Embedded and other devices.</span></li>
 <li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">A FlexNet ID Dongle for vendors who want a “belt and braces” approach to license management, combining software and hardware license management.</span></li>

<h3><b>Who is Flexera?</b></h3>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Flexera and its license management product, FlexLM Publisher, have a long history. The software was originally developed for UNIX environments in 1988 by GLOBEtrotter Software, and marketed by Highland Software, who held the rights until 1994, when they were bought by GLOBEtrotter. GLOBEtrotter was then purchased in 2000 by Macrovision Corp, who renamed the product from FlexLM to FlexNet Publisher. In its turn, in 2008 Macrovision sold its software division to Thomas Bravo, a private equity company, who renamed it to Acresso Software, but changed the name again in 2009 to Flexera Software, following a legal challenge by Agresso Software,  and the product name to FlexNet Publisher. In 2011 Teachers' Private Capital, the private investment</span><span style="font-weight: 400;"> department of the Ontario Teachers' Pension Plan, had agreed to acquire a majority stake in Flexera Software (size of stake undisclosed). </span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">Flexera has steadily been acquiring software companies to build its brand:-</span>
<ul>
 <li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">In 2010, it acquired ManageSoft, an Australian company, and its 250 enterprise customers using 3 million devices for its SAM capability.</span></li>
 <li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">In 2018 it acquired RightScale for its cloud management capability</span></li>
 <li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">In 2019 it acquired RISC Networks to complement its investment in Rightscale.</span></li>
</ul>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Flexera is a private Chicago-based company with over 1300 employees and over 50,000 customers managing 30 million devices. It is the world’s leading software license management provider and second only to Gemalto in license management because of Gemalto’s large base of hardware license management.</span>
