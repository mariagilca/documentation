## About Apollo.io

Apollo.io is a comprehensive sales intelligence and engagement platform that helps sales teams find, engage, and close more deals. It provides a vast database of contacts and companies, along with tools for outreach automation, email sequencing, and analytics. Monitoring Apollo.io usage is essential for optimizing subscription costs and ensuring that sales intelligence licenses are utilized effectively across your organization.
