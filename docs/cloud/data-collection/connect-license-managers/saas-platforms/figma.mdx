# Figma

Figma is a collaborative interface design tool used by designers, product managers, and developers. It allows teams to create, prototype, and share designs in a single, cloud-based platform.

## Before you begin

- An active OpenLM Platform tenant.
- Figma-side administrator access who can authorize the connection.

## How OpenLM monitors Figma

OpenLM Support configures the connection between your Figma account and your OpenLM tenant. Once activated, Figma usage data appears in OpenLM Platform alongside your other data sources, refreshed on the same cadence as the rest of your integrations.

:::info[Support assistance required]
OpenLM Support configures this integration for your tenant. Open a ticket from the [Customer Portal](https://customer.openlm.com) with:

- Your OpenLM tenant name
- Your Figma account ID
- Confirmation that a Figma administrator is available to authorize the connection
:::

## What you receive after activation

- Figma usage data in OpenLM Platform reports.
- Allocation data alongside your other data sources.
