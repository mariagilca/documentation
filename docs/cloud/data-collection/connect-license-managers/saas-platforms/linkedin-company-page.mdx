LinkedIn Company Page seats let marketing and sales teams manage an organization's LinkedIn presence. OpenLM monitors LinkedIn Company Page license usage through the OpenLM SaaS Agent. Detailed configuration steps are being prepared and will be published in the next documentation release.

## Before you begin

- An active OpenLM Platform tenant.
- OpenLM Cloud Broker, approved in [Broker Hub](/cloud/data-collection/broker-hub).
- Administrative access to your LinkedIn company page, so you can authorize access to user and license data.

:::note[Documentation in progress]
If you need to configure LinkedIn Company Page today, open a ticket from the [Customer Portal](https://customer.openlm.com) and reference this page. We will share the current procedure directly while the doc is finalized.
:::
