# Adobe Cloud

Adobe Creative Cloud is a suite of applications and services for graphic design, video editing, web development, and photography. It includes industry-standard tools like Photoshop, Illustrator, and Premiere Pro. Organizations monitor its usage to optimize subscription costs, ensure license compliance, and understand which creative teams are using which tools.

## Before you begin

- An active OpenLM Platform tenant.
- Adobe Cloud-side administrator access who can authorize the connection.

## How OpenLM monitors Adobe Cloud

OpenLM Support configures the connection between your Adobe Cloud account and your OpenLM tenant. Once activated, Adobe Cloud usage data appears in OpenLM Platform alongside your other data sources, refreshed on the same cadence as the rest of your integrations.

:::info[Support assistance required]
OpenLM Support configures this integration for your tenant. Open a ticket from the [Customer Portal](https://customer.openlm.com) with:

- Your OpenLM tenant name
- Your Adobe Cloud account ID
- Confirmation that a Adobe Cloud administrator is available to authorize the connection
:::

## What you receive after activation

- Adobe Cloud usage data in OpenLM Platform reports.
- Allocation data alongside your other data sources.
