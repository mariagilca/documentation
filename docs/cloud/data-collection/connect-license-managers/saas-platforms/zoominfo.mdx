ZoomInfo is a go-to-market intelligence platform that provides sales and marketing teams with business contact and company data. It helps users find, engage, and close deals.

## Before you begin

- An active OpenLM Platform tenant.
- ZoomInfo-side administrator access who can authorize the connection.

## How OpenLM monitors ZoomInfo

OpenLM Support configures the connection between your ZoomInfo account and your OpenLM tenant. Once activated, ZoomInfo usage data appears in OpenLM Platform alongside your other data sources, refreshed on the same cadence as the rest of your integrations.

:::info[Support assistance required]
OpenLM Support configures this integration for your tenant. Open a ticket from the [Customer Portal](https://customer.openlm.com) with:

- Your OpenLM tenant name
- Your ZoomInfo account ID
- Confirmation that a ZoomInfo administrator is available to authorize the connection
:::

## What you receive after activation

- ZoomInfo usage data in OpenLM Platform reports.
- Allocation data alongside your other data sources.
