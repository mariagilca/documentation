# Autodesk Cloud

Autodesk Cloud offers a suite of cloud-based design and make software for the architecture, engineering, construction, and manufacturing industries. It includes tools like Revit, AutoCAD, and Fusion 360.

## Before you begin

- An active OpenLM Platform tenant.
- Autodesk Cloud-side administrator access who can authorize the connection.

## How OpenLM monitors Autodesk Cloud

OpenLM Support configures the connection between your Autodesk Cloud account and your OpenLM tenant. Once activated, Autodesk Cloud usage data appears in OpenLM Platform alongside your other data sources, refreshed on the same cadence as the rest of your integrations.

:::info[Support assistance required]
OpenLM Support configures this integration for your tenant. Open a ticket from the [Customer Portal](https://customer.openlm.com) with:

- Your OpenLM tenant name
- Your Autodesk Cloud account ID
- Confirmation that a Autodesk Cloud administrator is available to authorize the connection
:::

## What you receive after activation

- Autodesk Cloud usage data in OpenLM Platform reports.
- Allocation data alongside your other data sources.
