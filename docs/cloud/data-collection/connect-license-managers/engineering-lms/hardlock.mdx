Follow these steps to configure OpenLM to monitor license usage and retrieve statistics from the Hardlock license manager.

## Before you begin

- OpenLM Platform
- OpenLM Broker v25.x or higher installed on the same machine as Hardlock, and approve it in [Broker Hub](/cloud/data-collection/broker-hub).

## Monitoring capabilities

The current monitoring capabilities for Hardlock through OpenLM Broker are:

| Report resolution | License totals | License usage | Denials reporting | Borrowed license reporting | Expiration date reporting | Multiple server redundancy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Log file parsing | Yes | Yes | No | No | No | No |

## Configure OpenLM Broker

![Diagram of how OpenLM interfaces with the Hardlock license manager](/img/engineering-lms/hardlock/image27.png)
*Diagram of how OpenLM interfaces with the Hardlock license manager*

:::note
OpenLM Broker must be installed and reporting to OpenLM Server before starting these steps.
:::
### Add manually
1. Open Broker UI at http://localhost:5090/.

1. Go to License Managers → Add License Manager.

    ![Select Hardlock, enter the port number, and click Add.](/img/engineering-lms/hardlock/image12.png)
*Select Hardlock, enter the port number, and click Add.*

    ![In Vendors, click Add Vendor, enter Hardlock, and click Continue.](/img/engineering-lms/hardlock/image153.png)
*In Vendors, click Add Vendor, enter Hardlock, and click Continue.*

    ![In Log Files, click Add Log File, browse to the .log file, and click Open.](/img/engineering-lms/hardlock/image167.png)
*In Log Files, click Add Log File, browse to the .log file, and click Open.*

1. Select the vendor Hardlock and click Continue.

    ![Click Save.](/img/engineering-lms/hardlock/image38.png)
*Click Save.*

## Approve Hardlock in OpenLM Platform

1. Sign in to OpenLM Platform.
2. From the menu, go to **License Servers** → **Pending Server**.
3. Select the Hardlock license manager, then select **Approve and Merge**.

## Verify the integration

1. In OpenLM Platform, go to **License Servers Live** → **Server Statistics**.
2. Confirm Hardlock appears with a green status indicator.

:::note
It can take up to 3 minutes for the status to update for a new connection.
:::
