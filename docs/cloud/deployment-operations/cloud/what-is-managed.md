- Control plane, data services, and routine upgrades are operated by OpenLM.
- High availability, backups, and scaling are handled by the service.
- You manage only your on-network agents and license managers.

## Customer responsibilities

- Install and maintain on-network components (Broker, Workstation Agent, DSA).
- Provide connectivity from Brokers/DSA to license managers and directories.
- Manage tenant access (users, groups, roles) and data sources.
