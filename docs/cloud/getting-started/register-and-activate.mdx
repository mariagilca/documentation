This page walks you through account registration, signing in, and product activation.

## Register for OpenLM

### Go to the registration page

1. Visit the [OpenLM free trial page](https://www.openlm.com/free-trial/).
2. In the **Signup for Cloud** card, choose your region:
   - **US Cloud** — recommended for global and other regions.
   - **EU Cloud** — recommended for organizations in Europe.
3. Select **Register Now**. The OpenLM Platform registration page opens.

### Select a registration method

You can register in one of two ways:

![The OpenLM Platform registration page, with social sign-in and direct registration options](/img/getting_started/identity-registration.png)
*The OpenLM Platform registration page*

- **Register with Socials** (Google, Microsoft, or GitHub):
  1. Select your provider.
  2. Authenticate using the provider's sign-in page.
  3. After authentication, you are signed in and land on the OpenLM Platform Homepage.

- **Direct Registration**:
  1. Enter your email address and create a password.
  2. Select **Register**.
  3. After registration, sign in with your email and password.
  4. You land on the OpenLM Platform Homepage.

## Activate products

New accounts start with no products activated. From the **Homepage**, activate the products you need — start with **Software License Management**, which powers the core license usage, denials, server-health, and expiration data.

![The Products page groups products into Active, Deactivated, Available, and System products sections](/img/getting_started/activate-products.png)
*The Products page, with Software License Management and Directory Sync activated*

<Checklist>
  <ChecklistItem title="Open the Products page">
    On the Homepage, select **Activate Product** in the toolbar (or **Activate SLM** on the dashboard lock card). You can also open the app launcher (grid icon, top-right) and select **Platform Administration → OpenLM Products**.
  </ChecklistItem>

  <ChecklistItem title="Activate Software License Management">
    In the **Available products** section, find **Software License Management**, select **Activate**, and confirm. Activation can take up to 10 minutes. Once active, the Homepage widgets begin to populate as your components report data.
  </ChecklistItem>

  <ChecklistItem title="Activate any other products you need">
    Activate additional products (for example, Directory Sync, Alerts, or Compliance) one at a time using the same **Activate** button. The Products page groups products into four sections:
    - **Active products** — products you have activated.
    - **Deactivated products** — products you activated and later turned off. Select **Reactivate** to restore one.
    - **Available products** — products ready to activate.
    - **System products** — core OpenLM products that are always active and cannot be deactivated.
  </ChecklistItem>
</Checklist>
