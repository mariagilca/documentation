# Register and activate

This page walks you through account registration, product activation, and Reporting service setup.

## Register for OpenLM

### Go to the registration page

1. Visit the [OpenLM website](https://www.openlm.com/free-trial/).
2. Under the **OpenLM Platform** section, click **Register now**.

### Choose a registration method

You can register in one of two ways:

![Identity registration screen](/img/getting_started/identity-registration.png)
*Identity registration screen*

- **Social login** (Google, Microsoft, or GitHub):
  1. Select your provider.
  2. Authenticate using the provider's login page.
  3. After authentication, you are redirected to the OpenLM homepage and onboarding guide.

- **Direct registration**:
  1. Enter your username and create a password.
  2. Click **Register**.
  3. After registration, click **Login** to access the system.
  4. You are redirected to the OpenLM homepage and onboarding guide.

## Activate products

<Checklist>
  <ChecklistItem title="Open the products page">
    Click **Activate products** in the onboarding guide to open the products page.
  </ChecklistItem>
  
  <ChecklistItem title="Activate your products">
    Select and activate products one by one. The products page includes the following sections:
    - **Active products**: Lists your activated licenses.
    - **Available products**: Shows products ready for activation.
    - **System products**: Displays pre-configured items that cannot be activated or deactivated.
  </ChecklistItem>
</Checklist>

## Set up the Reporting service (Amazon Quick Suite)

<Checklist>
  <ChecklistItem title="Wait for the Quick Suite invitation email">
    Once your OpenLM tenant is created, the system triggers an email invitation to join Amazon Quick Suite. This typically takes 5-10 minutes after registration. Check the inbox for the email address you used to register.
  </ChecklistItem>

  <ChecklistItem title="Open the invitation email">
    The email from Amazon Quick Suite contains a link to activate your reporting access. You cannot access the Reporting service until you complete this step.
  </ChecklistItem>

  <ChecklistItem title="Register for Quick Suite">
    Click the link in the invitation email. On the Quick Suite sign-up page, if prompted for an account name, enter: **openlm** (all lowercase).
  </ChecklistItem>

  <ChecklistItem title="Sign in to Quick Suite">
    Sign in with the same email address where you received the invitation. This should match your OpenLM registration email.
  </ChecklistItem>

  <ChecklistItem title="Open the Reporting service">
    After signing in, click the **OpenLM Reporting** link in the top navigation bar.
  </ChecklistItem>
</Checklist>
