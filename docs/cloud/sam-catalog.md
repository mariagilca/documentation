**SAM Catalog** gives your organization a centralized, structured catalog of the software applications it owns and uses. It complements [Software Asset Management](/cloud/sam) — where SAM records the commercial side (sellers, purchases, and entitlement records), the catalog is the normalized inventory of the applications themselves, so entitlements, usage, and procurement all refer to the same, consistent set of products.

:::info[Finding this in the app]
Open the OpenLM Platform **app launcher** (grid icon, top-right) and select **Software Asset Management → SAM Catalog**.

**Before you start:** Activate **Software Asset Management** in [OpenLM Products](/cloud/openlm-administration/products).
:::

:::note
SAM Catalog is a newer capability, available as a trial on the OpenLM Cloud Platform. Availability depends on your plan and account activation.
:::

## What the catalog is for

- **A single source of product names.** A structured list of applications, so the same product is not recorded under several spellings across entitlements, usage, and reports.
- **Automatic enrichment.** Products that OpenLM observes but cannot match to a catalog entry are enriched automatically, reducing the manual work of keeping the catalog complete and consistent.
- **A foundation for Software Asset Management.** Entitlement records and reporting reference catalog entries, so cost, compliance, and utilization are attributed to the right application.
