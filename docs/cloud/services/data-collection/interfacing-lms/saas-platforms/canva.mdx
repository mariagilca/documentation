## About Canva

Canva is a versatile online design and visual communication platform that empowers users to create professional graphics, presentations, social media posts, and more. With its intuitive drag-and-drop interface and extensive template library, Canva is widely used by marketing teams, designers, and content creators. Monitoring Canva usage is crucial for optimizing subscription costs and ensuring that design licenses are allocated to active users.
