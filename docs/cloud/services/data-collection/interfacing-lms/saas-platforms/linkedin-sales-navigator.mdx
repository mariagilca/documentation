# LinkedIn Sales Navigator
LinkedIn Sales Navigator Usage Monitoring

## About LinkedIn Sales Navigator

LinkedIn Sales Navigator is a premium tool for sales professionals that helps them find the right prospects, understand key insights, and engage with potential customers. Monitoring Sales Navigator usage is important for ensuring that sales teams are leveraging the platform effectively and that licenses are not being wasted. This is similar to monitoring other sales tools like Salesforce or Pipedrive.

## Prerequisites

To begin monitoring LinkedIn Sales Navigator, ensure you have the following components and permissions in place:

- OpenLM Components: OpenLM Cloud Broker and OpenLM SLM are required. For automation, consider OpenLM OneDirectorySync or License Allocation Control.
- Platform-specific Requirements: You'll need administrative access to your LinkedIn Sales Navigator account to generate an API key and obtain the necessary permissions.
