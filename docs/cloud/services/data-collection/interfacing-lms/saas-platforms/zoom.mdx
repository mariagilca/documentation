## About Zoom

Zoom is a leading video conferencing and communication platform that enables virtual meetings, webinars, chat, and collaboration. It has become an essential tool for remote work, online education, and virtual events. Monitoring Zoom usage is important for optimizing subscription costs, understanding meeting patterns, and ensuring that licenses are allocated to users who need them most.
