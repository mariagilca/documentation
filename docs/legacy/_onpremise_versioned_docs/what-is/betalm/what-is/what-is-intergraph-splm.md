### What is Intergraph SPLM?

**Intergraph SPLM (SmartPlant License Manager)** is an in-house developed license manager for **SmartPlant** installations. Designed for concurrent usage, it runs as a background service with the main executable `license.exe`.

OpenLM provides the following functionality for monitoring SPLM licenses:

* **Denials Reporting**: No
* **Report resolution**: By Minute
* **Borrowed License reporting**: No
* **Expiration Date reporting**: No
* **Multiple Server Redundancy Support**: No
* **Token License Support**: No

Intergraph SPLM is one of over 70 license managers that OpenLM can manage.

***

### Who is Intergraph?

**Intergraph**, now known as **Hexagon PPM** after its acquisition by Hexagon in 2010, specializes in plant management software, particularly for the oil and gas industries. Founded in 1969 as M & S Computing, the company initially focused on graphics software and became a leading CAD systems vendor. After transitioning away from hardware, Intergraph developed its flagship software, **SmartPlant 3D**, for the design, construction, and maintenance of industrial plants. The dedicated license manager for this software, **Intergraph SPLM**, was developed by Intergraph CAS.
