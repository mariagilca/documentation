### What is BetaLM?

BetaLM is a **proprietary license manager** developed by **Beta CAE Systems** to manage its suite of Computer-Aided Engineering (CAE) products, such as the **ANSA pre-processor** and the **META post-processor**. This simulation software is often used in **batch mode**.

OpenLM provides the following functionality for monitoring BetaLM licenses:

* **Denials Reporting**: Yes
* **Report resolution**: By second
* **Borrowed License reporting**: Not applicable
* **Expiration Date reporting**: Yes
* **Multiple Server Redundancy Support**: Triad
* **Token License Support**: No

### Who is Beta CAE Systems?

**BETA CAE Systems** is a Swiss CAE software company founded over 25 years ago in Lucerne. The company has since expanded globally with offices in the US, India, Japan, and Europe. Its primary products, the **ANSA pre-processor** and **META post-processor**, are widely used in the automotive, aerospace, and petrochemical industries.

The company developed its own proprietary license manager, BetaLM, for its software. BETA CAE's simulation software is optimized to run on both Nvidia and AMD GPUs, and ANSA can accept data formats from other CAE vendors like Dassault and Siemens.
