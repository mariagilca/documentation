### What is LM-X?

**LM-X** is license management software developed by **X-Formation**, a Danish company. It is a cross-platform solution, able to run on **Windows**, **Linux**, and **Mac OS**, as well as various open-source platforms. The software supports a range of licensing models, including **network**, **node-locked**, **token-based**, and **pay-per-use** licensing. LM-X can also integrate with **HASP dongles** for customers who want both hardware and software license protection. It also supports license borrowing and grace periods.

OpenLM provides the following functionality for monitoring LM-X licenses:

* **Denials Reporting**: Yes
* **Report resolution**: By Minute
* **Borrowed License reporting**: No
* **Expiration Date reporting**: Yes
* **Multiple Server Redundancy Support**: No
* **Token License Support**: Yes

LM-X is one of over 70 license managers that OpenLM can manage.

***

### Who is X-Formation?

**X-Formation** is a small, independent Danish software vendor that provides its license management software, **LM-X**, to approximately 600 customers. Founded in 2004, the company has its headquarters in Aarhus, Denmark, with its development team located in Krakow, Poland. Although a small organization with around 25 employees, X-Formation has a list of large clients, including **ABB**, **LUK**, and **NASA**. In addition to LM-X, the company also offers a license activation and statistical reporting product.
