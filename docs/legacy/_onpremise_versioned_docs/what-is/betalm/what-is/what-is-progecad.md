### What is ProgeCAD?

ProgeCAD is the premier product from **Progesoft**, a company that is a founding member of the **IntelliCAD Technology Consortium**. Marketed as ProgeCAD Professional, ProgeCAD Architecture, and iCADMac for Apple Mac users, it is a **2D/3D CAD application** that offers high compatibility with **AutoCAD** at a competitive price.

A key differentiator from modern Autodesk products is that ProgeCAD offers **perpetual licensing**, allowing customers to use the software indefinitely without an annual fee. For users who want automatic updates, an annual maintenance plan called **Icare Services** is available. Licenses can be purchased for single users or as network licenses with various permutations (e.g., per site, per country).

### Who is Progesoft?

**Progesoft** is a Swiss company with a long history in CAD software, starting in 1985 by supplying software to ABB. In 1999, it became one of the founding members of the **IntelliCAD Technology Consortium**, a group of software developers who collaborate on a `.dwg`-compatible CAD platform. This allows Progesoft to compete directly with AutoCAD.

With an impressive customer list that includes **Airbus**, **Honda**, **NASA**, and **Samsung**, Progesoft's products are known for their compatibility with AutoCAD and Revit, making them a useful addition to many software portfolios. The company's core products are **progeCAD Professional**, **progeCAD Architecture**, and **iCADMac**.
