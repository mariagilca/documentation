### What is Adobe Creative Cloud (Adobe CC)?

Adobe Creative Cloud (CC) is a **SaaS (Software as a Service)** offering that bundles over 30 of Adobe's graphics and creative applications into a single cloud subscription. Adobe was a pioneer in moving from a perpetual licensing model to a subscription-based one, a trend that was later adopted by many other software vendors like Autodesk.

Hosted on **Microsoft Azure**, CC includes popular applications such as **Acrobat Pro**, **Photoshop**, **InDesign**, and **Dreamweaver**. There are different versions of CC for individuals, businesses, and enterprises. For those who primarily need document management, there's also the **Document Cloud**.

OpenLM can monitor Adobe CC licenses as one of its supported vendors. For Adobe CC, OpenLM provides reporting on expiration dates and a report resolution by the minute.

***

### What is Adobe Document Cloud (Adobe DC)?

Adobe Document Cloud is a subscription-based service focused on **document management**, with a particular emphasis on PDF tools and digital signatures. While it's primarily for enterprises, individual and SME versions are also available. It's designed to integrate with both Creative Cloud and Experience Cloud.

For Adobe Document Cloud, OpenLM offers the same monitoring functionalities as for Creative Cloud, including reporting on expiration dates and a report resolution by the minute.

***

### What is Adobe Experience Cloud?

Adobe Experience Cloud is a comprehensive software bundle for **managing and measuring the customer experience**. It was launched in 2017, combining Adobe's digital marketing, advertising, and analytics products. Like the other Adobe cloud offerings, it integrates with Creative Cloud and Document Cloud.

Some of the products within the Experience Cloud include **Audience Manager**, **Campaign**, and **Target**.

OpenLM also monitors Adobe Experience Cloud licenses, providing functionality for expiration date reporting and a report resolution by the minute.
