### What is Office 365?

**Office 365** is **Microsoft's** **Software as a Service (SaaS)** offering. While perpetual licenses like Office 2019 are still available, Microsoft is encouraging customers to adopt Office 365 by bundling additional cloud-based products and services. These include collaboration tools like **Microsoft Teams** and scheduling applications like **Microsoft Bookings**. The Office 365 product range is segmented into licenses for individuals, businesses (up to 250 employees), and enterprises.

As of **Release 4.5**, OpenLM has added the capability to manage Microsoft 365 licenses in the cloud. OpenLM provides the following functionality for monitoring Office 365 licenses:

* **Denials Reporting**: No
* **Report resolution**: By Minute
* **Borrowed License reporting**: Not applicable
* **Expiration Date reporting**: Yes
* **Multiple Server Redundancy Support**: Not applicable
* **Token License Support**: No

Office 365 is one of over 30 license managers that OpenLM supports.
