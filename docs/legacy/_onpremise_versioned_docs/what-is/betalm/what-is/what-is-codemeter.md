### About Codemeter

**Codemeter** is a software licensing and security solution from the German company **Wibu-Systems**. Wibu specializes in secure hardware license managers, such as dongles, and has a reputation for reliability and security. While their primary market is hardware-based, they also offer a variety of software licensing options.

OpenLM provides the following functionality for monitoring Codemeter licenses:

* **Denials Reporting**: Yes
* **Report resolution**: By Minute
* **Borrowed License reporting**: No
* **Expiration Date reporting**: Yes
* **Multiple Server Redundancy Support**: No
* **Token License Support**: No

Codemeter is one of more than 60 license managers supported by OpenLM.

### Who is Wibu?

**Wibu-Systems** is a German company that has specialized in software licensing and security since its founding in 1989. The company's flagship product, **Codemeter**, is used by over 7,000 independent software vendors globally. Wibu is highly regarded by industry analysts for its reliable and secure solutions, which are resistant to piracy and cyberattacks.

Wibu’s reputation for security is built on a foundation of innovative technology, including their patented "Blurry Box" cryptography and the use of the AES 256-bit public encryption algorithm. The company has demonstrated the strength of its technology through public hackathons where no one has been able to crack their product.

Headquartered in Karlsruhe, Germany, Wibu-Systems has a global presence with subsidiaries in the United States, China, and Japan, as well as sales offices across Europe.
