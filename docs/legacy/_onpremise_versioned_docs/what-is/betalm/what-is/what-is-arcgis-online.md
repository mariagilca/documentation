### What is ArcGIS Online? 

**ArcGIS Online** is the **Software as a Service (SaaS)** version of Esri's ArcGIS product line. It provides a cloud-based platform for geographic information system (GIS) professionals and teams to create, manage, analyze, and share geographic data.

Instead of traditional perpetual licenses, ArcGIS Online uses **"User Types"** that are based on a user's role, such as "GIS professional" and "Fieldworker." While "Creator" roles have administrative permissions, even a view-only "Viewer" role requires a license. 

ArcGIS Online offers benefits like automatic software updates, no maintenance fees, and easy sharing of work through the cloud. It's available on three different cloud ecosystems: Amazon Web Services (AWS), Microsoft Azure, or Esri's own cloud.

### Who is ESRI?

The **Environmental Systems Research Institute, Inc. (Esri)** is an American multinational GIS software company. Founded in 1969 by Jack and Laura Dangermond, it initially began as a consulting firm focused on spatial analysis. 

Esri released its first commercial GIS software, **ArcGIS**, in 1981, and it has since become the most widely used GIS software globally. The company, still a privately-owned family business, has grown to employ thousands of people with offices worldwide and serves over a million users.
