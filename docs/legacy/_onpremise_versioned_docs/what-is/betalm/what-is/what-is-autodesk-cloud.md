### What is Autodesk Cloud?

Autodesk has transitioned from selling perpetual licenses to a subscription-based model. While many vendors offer cloud subscriptions as a straightforward alternative to on-premise software, Autodesk's approach is more complex. Their cloud offerings can be standalone solutions like **Fusion 360** for manufacturing or additional products that require an existing subscription to related software. For example, **Autodesk Rendering** is only available to subscribers of certain Autodesk software.

A further layer of complexity is the use of **Cloud Credits**, a form of consumption token needed to "pay" for certain cloud services. For large organizations, managing these credits and assigning them to the right users can be a significant administrative task.

As of July 2019, Autodesk offered a variety of cloud products, though this list changes frequently. Some notable categories include:

* **Collaboration Products**: AutoCAD Mobile App and Recap (Reality Capture) Pro.
* **Building and Construction**: BIM 360, Formit, and Insight.
* **Product Design and Manufacturing**: Fusion 360, Fusion Team, and Configurator 360.
* **Rendering**: Autodesk Rendering, which is used by professionals in AEC and manufacturing.

OpenLM provides specific monitoring functionality for Autodesk Cloud licenses:

* **Report resolution**: By minute.
* **Expiration Date reporting**: Yes.
* **Token License Support**: Yes.

### What are Cloud Credits from Autodesk?

**Cloud Credits** are a consumption token used to pay for certain Autodesk cloud services. They are different from **Token-Flex** tokens but serve a similar function. Credits are either provided with a software subscription or can be purchased directly from Autodesk.

There are two main types of credits:

* **Starter Credits**: These are provided with a software purchase, are non-transferable, and can only be used by the named user.
* **Purchased Credits**: These can be bought from the Autodesk Store and are sharable among users.

Cloud credits have a maximum lifespan of 12 months, and all credits expire when the associated contract ends. This requires careful management, especially when topping up credits.
