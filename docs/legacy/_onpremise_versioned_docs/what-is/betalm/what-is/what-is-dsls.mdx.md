### What is DSLS?

**DSLS (Dassault Systemes License Server)** is a proprietary license management application from **Dassault Systemes**. Its main function is to enroll and manage Dassault licenses on a server, granting or denying access to client applications based on license availability and compliance.

Historically, DSLS did not support virtual servers due to security concerns. However, this policy was updated in 2018, and DSLS now supports virtualization, which simplifies life for administrators in virtualized environments.

OpenLM provides the following functionality for monitoring DSLS licenses:

* **Denials Reporting**: Yes
* **Report resolution**: By Minute
* **Borrowed License reporting**: Yes
* **Expiration Date reporting**: Yes
* **Multiple Server Redundancy Support**: Yes
* **Token License Support**: Yes

DSLS is one of more than 70 license managers that OpenLM can manage.

### Who is Dassault?

**Dassault Systemes**, a French company founded in the 1970s, originated from the aviation sector with its iconic Mirage aircraft. Through a partnership with IBM in the late 1970s to develop **CATIA**, it has evolved into a major engineering software vendor.

Dassault Systemes has a strong focus on **3D modeling** within the **Product Lifecycle Management (PLM)** value chain, known as the "3D Experience." The company has over 250,000 enterprise customers and a vast user base. Its product portfolio includes:

* **3DExcite**
* **3DExperience**
* **CATIA**
* **BioVia**
* **Simulia** (formerly Abaqus)
* **Solidworks**
