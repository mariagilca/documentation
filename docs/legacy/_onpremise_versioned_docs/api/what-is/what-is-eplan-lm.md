<h2><b>What is EPLAN LM?</b></h2>

<h3><b>Who is EPLAN?</b></h3>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">EPLAN is one of the companies in the German family-owned Friedhelm Loh Group and was established in 1984. Their software supports process engineering in a number of engineering disciplines, especially electrical, automation, and mechatronics. EPLAN’s range of software products helps customers standardize and optimize the many disparate software products and artifacts used in a PLM or PDM environment. Apart from the EPLAN Platform and Engineering configuration, which help companies streamline their engineering process flows, EPLAN offers applications tailored to particular engineering challenges, such as Electric P8 for electrical engineering flows, Harness ProD for the design and management of cable harnesses and Fluid, which assists in fluid power engineering.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">EPLAN employs 1000 employees and has 55,000 users globally.</span>
