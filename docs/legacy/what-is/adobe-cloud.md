## What is Adobe Cloud?

Adobe Creative Cloud (CC) is a SaaS offering that bundles all of Adobe's graphics software into a single cloud offering, which is hosted on Microsoft Azure. Adobe is notorious for being the first major software vendor to offer a subscription-based software model, replacing their perpetual licensing model, and starting the trend among other vendors, such as Autodesk.

There are various forms of Creative Cloud, targeted at individual, business, and enterprise customers. Some of the over 30 products bundled in CC include Acrobat Pro, Photoshop, InDesign, and Dreamweaver. For customers who do not need access to graphics, but require the document management capability of Acrobat, there is a Document Cloud option. There is also a marketing/customer experience offering, called Experience Cloud.

## What is Adobe Document Cloud (Adobe DC)?

Adobe Document Cloud is a subscription-based service primarily for enterprises that need to use the full capability of Acrobat, including digital signatures, although individual and SME versions are also available. It integrates both with Creative and Experience Cloud.

## What is Adobe Experience Cloud?

Adobe Experience Cloud was announced in 2017 and combines the products formerly known as Adobe Marketing Cloud, Advertising Cloud, and Analytics Cloud to provide a comprehensive software bundle for managing and measuring the customer experience. It integrates with Creative Cloud and Document Cloud. Some of the products included in the Experience bouquet include Audience Manager, Campaign, and Target.
