## What is EasyCopy?

EasyCopy is a suite of products for imaging, plotting, and editing graphical images of all formats. Products include EasyPlot, EasyView, and JPEG PRO. EasyCopy has a proprietary license manager which can be monitored by OpenLM, which is one of EasyCopy's partners.

OpenLM provides the following functionality for monitoring EasyCopy licenses:

- Denials reporting *No*
- Report resolution *By second*
- Borrowed license reporting *Yes*
- Expiration date reporting *Yes*
- Multiple server redundancy support *No*
- Token license support *No*

EasyCopy is only one of over 70 license managers that OpenLM can manage. [Check the list](https://www.openlm.com/license-manager-capabilities/).

### Who is EasyCopy?

The EasyCopy Company was formerly known as Augrin software, which was merged with its sister company Autograph International and renamed in 2007, after its flagship product, EasyCopy. The company specializes in software for imaging and printing complex, large and specialized images, such as well logging in the upstream oil and gas industry (Easycore). EasyCopy was founded some 30 years ago in Denmark and opened a US branch in Houston shortly afterward.

OpenLM is a partner of EasyCopy and is integrated with the EasyCopy products.
